# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be direct and be respectful — they're not in tension. Critique code and decisions
plainly; don't make it personal. Assume good faith; ask before assuming malice or
incompetence.

## Expected

- Constructive, specific feedback in reviews.
- Disagreement about technical decisions, argued on merits.
- Respect for contributors' and users' time, data, and privacy.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or conduct, in any project
  space (issues, PRs, discussions).
- Deliberately submitting insecure, malicious, or deceptive code.
- Misrepresenting AI-generated work as human-authored or vice versa — see
  [`AI_ASSISTED_ENGINEERING.md`](AI_ASSISTED_ENGINEERING.md).

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to **info@shatasolutions.com**. Reports are handled
confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainers determine consequences proportional to the violation, up to and
including removal from the project.
